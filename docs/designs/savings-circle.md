## Problem Statement

In the owner's words: "model how some African communities have savings circles where funds pile up in the shared account and they can use it for emergency funds or give loans to members especially when they aren't able to obtain loans via traditional banks."

The model is an accumulating savings group (CARE-style VSLA), not a rotating ROSCA. Every member pays a fixed amount per cycle. Money leaves the pot only as a loan, an emergency grant, or an end-of-period share-out. The DAO decides every loan by vote. Tribe Builder already has the vote (Governor) and the badge (Token); what is missing is a members' pot the DAO cannot spend, the passbook, the loan ledger, and the cap that keeps a vote from over-lending.

## What Makes This Cool

The passbook is the credit line. A member's paid months are their collateral: the contract caps a loan at `k × (stamps still in the pot) × dues`, so a group can lend to someone no bank will, with no upfront deposit, and the share-out at the end of the period is where a defaulter's savings are forfeited. Every stamp, loan, repayment, and pledge lands in the DAO feed as a sentence, so the whole group reads the same passbook. Guarantor pledges, the social collateral real circles run on, are the first follow-up (deferred from v1, RD-2).

There is no platform minter.

This plan's `DaoModule` means that trait.

- Payment assets are Stellar asset contracts (SEP-41). `approve(from, spender, amount, expiration_ledger)` overwrites and expires silently.

## Premises

Revised by the CEO review (2026-10-13); the decision rows are in the CEO Review ledger.

1. VSLA model with **two pots** (CEO-1): members' savings, repayments, and interest live in the Circle pot and come back at share-out by right; the DAO Treasury keeps every other DAO fund and can donate into the pot but never withdraw. Nothing rotates.
2. Loans are ordinary Governor proposals (CEO-4): the Treasury calls `circle.disburse`, which enforces the cap and pays from the pot.
3. Badge = the DAO token, one per member, minted by the Circle on `join` within the mint allowance the DAO grants. Stamps = a non-voting per-member record of paid cycles inside the Circle; they drive the cap, the share-out, and the passbook. Voters and savers are kept the same by the savings-only preset and locked tokens (SC-1 to SC-5).
4. Enforcement is a savings-backed cap plus forfeit: the cap is `k × (borrower's paid stamps in the open period) × dues − outstanding`, a loan is due before the period closes, a member with an open loan cannot claim, and a defaulter forfeits every unclaimed stamp. Governance sets `k` and can grant a one-loan override (RD-3). Guarantor pledges are deferred (RD-2).
5. Members pay themselves with `pay_dues`; automatic collection is deferred (RD-1). Each payment carries a governance-set social part for the Treasury (CEO-3).
6. One payment asset per Circle; fixed dues and social fee per cycle, changed by governance between cycles.

## Cross-Model Perspective

A fresh Claude subagent (Codex not installed) read a summary of the session without the conversation. Its cold read:

- Coolest version: "The passbook is the credit line, and the badge shows it." Cap loans at `k × paid_stamps × dues` (CARE's rule is 3× savings), forfeit savings on default, add `guarantee(loan_id, guarantor)` so a member pledges part of their share-out behind another's loan.
- What excites the owner: "give loans to members especially when they aren't able to obtain loans via traditional banks." The ledger is a means; credit access is the point.
- 50% already built: `Marketplace.buy_primary` (pay the Treasury, then `token.mint` under `authorize`) is `join()` almost verbatim; the Minter's per-token persistent keys with `extend_persistent` are the stamp pattern; `Treasury.execute` already dispatches `circle.open_loan` as a proposal action with no Treasury change.
- Challenged premise 4 (High): "no collateral" is wrong for a VSLA; the member's own savings back the loan. The owner accepted the revision.
- Recommendation: build the stamp bitmap and the `open_loan` cap first.

Where this design differs: stamps are not pushed into Metadata traits (that needs a Circle-writable trait path in Metadata; deferred to a later release), and the bitmap starts at the member's joining cycle, not the circle's start.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Passbook Circle (minimal)
One contract with join, pay_dues, capped open_loan, repay; a bitmap passbook; indexer rows and a Circle tab. Share-out deferred, so forfeit is only a recorded status. Ruled out by the owner: the forfeit has to be real money.

### Approach B: Full VSLA cycle (chosen)
Approach A plus periods with a funded share-out claimed per member, forfeited stamps excluded, interest per loan, guarantor pledges, suspend/remove, and permissionless allowance collection.

### Approach C: Marketplace-as-dues
Monthly primary listings as dues, tokens as stamps, no contract changes. Ruled out: every stamp is a vote and the cap is advisory, which breaks premises 3 and 4.

## Recommended Approach

### Sequencing (SEQ-1)

By then the Token registry and allowances, Manager `create_module` / `predict_module_address` / `module_count`, the `DaoModule` trait (`launch`, `handoff`, `renewal_keys`), the registry-driven renewal sweep, and the §7 indexer (`dao_circles`, registration-windowed `manager.module_events`) all exist. ### Two pots (CEO-1, CEO-3)

```
  member pays dues ─┬── savings part ──────────────► CIRCLE POT (members own it, pro-rata by stamps)
                    │                                 in:  savings, repayments, interest, donations
                    │                                 out: loans (DAO vote), share-out (by right)
                    └── social part (default 0) ───► DAO TREASURY (the DAO's own money)
                                                      in:  social part, auction / pool / claim fees, donations
                                                      out: any passed proposal (emergency grants, ...)
  Treasury ──donate──► Circle pot      (never the other way: no proposal can withdraw savings)
```

- **The Circle pot** holds the savings part of every payment, loan repayments with their interest, and donations. Money leaves it only as a loan the DAO voted (`disburse`) or as a member's share-out claim. There is no admin withdrawal, no `fund_share_out`, no settlement, and no `sweep_residual`.
- **The Treasury** keeps everything else the DAO owns. `pay_dues` sends the governance-set `social_fee` (default 0) to the Treasury in the same call; emergency grants are ordinary Treasury proposals.
- **Treasury into the pot:** `donate(from, amount)` (anyone; the Treasury by proposal) adds to the pot and is shared out with the period. Lending capital into the pot with repayment to the Treasury was part of the CEO-1 answer; its loss-priority rule is open (CEO-5, Open Questions), so v1 ships `donate` only.

### Module shape

A new crate `contracts/circle`: a per-DAO module of kind `circle`, `non_voting = false` (it never holds NFTs; it mints them straight to members).

**Attaching a Circle to a live DAO is one proposal with three actions** (membership-models §2, §5):

1. `manager.create_module(token, circle, CircleConfig, expected_n)`: deploys the registered `circle` implementation at `predict_module_address(token, circle, n)`; the Token is Live, so the Manager immediately calls the Circle's `DaoModule::launch(treasury, open)` and it is Live with the Treasury as admin. A stale `expected_n` reverts the whole proposal (`StaleModuleIndex`).
2. `token.register_module(circle, <address>, false)`.
3. `token.increase_mint_allowance(<address>, N)`: the number of badges the DAO will issue through the Circle.

A savings-only DAO (SC-1) gets its Circle at creation instead: `create_dao` with a `ModuleSpec::Circle(CircleConfig)` (a new `ModuleSpec` variant), launched by `launch_dao` with the other modules. `join` refuses before the Token is Live (`NotLive`).

**The allowance is the membership cap.** `join` mints through `token.mint(circle, member)`, spending one unit; at zero the Token refuses with `AllowanceExceeded` and the whole `join` reverts, dues included. The Circle tab shows "N badges left"; raising it is a proposal.

**Upgrades and admin** follow every module: `common::upgrade`, admin = launch admin in Setup and the Treasury once handed over, destinations limited to Manager-approved `circle` hashes. Because the Circle now custodies the members' pot (CEO-1), that upgrade path is what members trust: a DAO vote to a hash the platform approved. SECURITY_MODEL states this.

**One Circle per DAO** (`KindTaken`). `payment_asset`, `cycle_secs`, `start_at`, and `cycles_per_period` are immutable. Replacing a Circle is a wind-down: pause it, let loans close or default and members claim, then `unregister_module(old)` and attach the new one. Unregistering earlier would hide the old Circle's later events (the indexer drops a module's events after `ModuleUnregistered`).

**Blast radius.** A Circle bug can mint up to its remaining allowance and can lose the pot. Both are bounded by the DAO's choices: the allowance it granted and the hashes it upgrades to.

### Membership: an address, a badge, and locked tokens (SC-1 to SC-5)

`Member` is keyed by the member's address; the badge is a DAO token. Who votes matters because every loan is a full DAO vote (CEO-4), so the plan makes voters and savers the same people:

- **Savings-only DAO preset (SC-1, G1).** A creation preset where the Circle is the DAO's only module with a mint allowance and founders join through the Circle (no founder batch mint). No custody modules are registered in the preset. The preset fixes `proposal_threshold = 1` so any member can propose their own loan (S1-2).
- **Locked tokens and membership switches, settings for any DAO (SC-3, SC-5).** `docs/designs/locked-tokens.md` owns the rule; this is a summary. `InitialDaoConfigValues.token_settings: TokenSettings { transfers_locked, members_only_delegation, dao_recovery }`, all off by default, each changeable by proposal in either direction (by the launch admin before launch). On a locked token a transfer succeeds only through a DAO-controlled path:
  1. out of DAO custody: `from` is the Treasury or a registered `non_voting` module (auction settlement, pool sale, `sweep_pool`);
  2. back to the DAO: `to` is the Treasury (a member gives up membership);
  3. a DAO-approved transfer: `transfer_from` with the Treasury as spender, after the holder `approve`d the Treasury for that token (holder consent plus a proposal);
  4. a DAO move: `dao_transfer(token_id, to)`, admin only, only while `dao_recovery` is on (on locked and unlocked tokens alike), taking the token from its current owner, never from or into a module (event `DaoTransfer`);
  5. a transfer agent: `agent_transfer(agent, from, to, token_id)`, which requires `agent` to be a registered module the DAO named with `set_transfer_agent` (the Circle), `agent` authorised and `from` authorised (event `AgentTransfer`).

  Everything else fails with `TransfersLocked`: holder-to-holder transfers, a holder's `approve` / `approve_for_all` to anyone but the Treasury, and Marketplace secondary `list`. With `members_only_delegation` on, a holder can delegate only to themselves or to another voting member (`DelegateNotMember`).
- **Changing address (SC-2).** `circle.move_membership(old, new)`, signed by both, re-keys the `Member` record (stamps, window, open loan, last claimed period), replaces the address in its `MemberPage`, updates the open `Loan.borrower`, and calls `token.agent_transfer(circle, old, new, token_id)` (the attach proposal also runs `token.set_transfer_agent(circle, true)`). `new` must have no `Member` record. Event `MembershipMoved`.
- **Lost keys (SC-4).** Members first rotate signers on the same address (classic account signers, contract-wallet key rotation). If that is impossible, a proposal runs `token.dao_transfer(token_id, new)` (recovery must be on; the same proposal may turn it on) plus `circle.recover_membership(old, new)` (Treasury-auth, same re-keying as `move_membership`). The same `dao_transfer` power can recall a badge: SECURITY_MODEL states it, and the web flags every proposal containing `dao_transfer` or turning recovery on.
- **Residuals, accepted.** Unless the DAO turns on `members_only_delegation` (the savings-only preset does), a holder can delegate their vote to anyone (the web shows delegations). Left, Removed, and Defaulted members keep their badge unless the DAO moves it with `dao_transfer`.

**Admission (S3-1).** Joining needs the DAO's say: a proposal publishes an intake round with `circle.set_intake(root, tree_uri)` (one live round; a new round replaces it), and `join(member, proof)` verifies the member's leaf with the ClaimMinter leaf and proof format (amount fixed at 1). A savings-only DAO's founders come from the round in its `CircleConfig`. The web fetches `tree_uri` and recomputes the root before offering `join`.

`join` refuses an address that already has a `Member` record unless its status is Left (then it reactivates the record without a second badge). A Removed address comes back only through `reinstate`. Whether `join` mints for an address that already holds a badge is Open Question 2.

### Loans and the cap (CEO-4, RD-2, RD-3)

A loan is a Governor proposal with one action, `circle.disburse(borrower, principal, due_at)`, executed by the Treasury; the Circle pays the borrower from the pot. Latency is the DAO's Governor settings (each delay 300 s to 30 days).

Cap, evaluated at execution: `principal ≤ k × open_stamps(borrower) × dues − outstanding(borrower)`, where `open_stamps` is the borrower's paid stamps in the open period. With an override set by a prior action in the same proposal: `principal ≤ override − outstanding`, consumed by that `disburse`. Every loan, override included, is also bounded by the loan ceiling (S3-2, S3-3): `principal ≤ max_loan_bps × slot(open).cash` (default 2,500 = 25%), else `LoanTooLarge`; `set_max_loan_bps` takes effect when the next period starts, so one proposal cannot raise the ceiling and drain the period. `disburse` also refuses when the open period's cash (`slot(open).cash`) is below `principal` (`PotTooLow`). `due_at` must fall on or before the open period's last cycle. Interest is flat: `outstanding = principal + principal × interest_bps / 10_000` at disbursement, and repayments go to the pot, so interest is the members' return. A member with an open loan cannot claim any share-out until it is repaid.

The "Request a loan" draft reads the chain, shows the live cap, the pot's free cash, and the latest allowed `due_at`, and warns when the vote would end after the period closes.

**Trustlines (S2-1).** A member must hold a trustline for the payment asset: `join` moves their savings, so it fails without one. `join`, `disburse`, and `claim_share` call the asset with `try_transfer` and turn a failure into `NoTrustline`; the loan draft and the claim button check the trustline before signing.

Who bears a loss: the forfeit recovers the defaulter's own savings in the pot; the rest of a defaulted principal reduces the pot, and so every other member's share-out. `k`, interest, and the override are the group's levers.

### Periods, share-out, and forfeit (S1-1: the period ring)

Period `p` covers cycles `[p × cycles_per_period, (p + 1) × cycles_per_period)`; `cycles_per_period ≤ 64`. Only three periods are ever live: the open one, the closing one, and the last claimable one. They live in a fixed ring of three slots inside `Config`, `slot = p % 3`, each `{p, total_stamps, open_loans, cash, payout, claimed, closed}`. There are no persistent per-period keys.

```
 money in/out                    slot it touches
 pay_dues / join (savings part)  the slot of the cycle being paid (always the open period)
 donate                          the open slot
 disburse                        the open slot: cash -= principal, open_loans += 1 (PotTooLow if cash < principal)
 repay                           the loan's slot while it is not closed; otherwise the open slot
 close_period(p)                 payout = slot(p).cash (an exact snapshot of p's own money)
 claim_share(member, p)          slot(p).claimed += share
 new period q starts             slot(q) is reused: if it held q-3, that period expires and its
                                 payout - claimed moves into slot(q).cash; if it held an unclosed
                                 period, PeriodBacklog
```

`close_period(p)` (anyone) requires the period's last cycle plus `grace_secs` to have passed, `p − 1` closed, and `slot(p).open_loans == 0` (overdue loans are first defaulted by anyone calling `mark_default`). It sets `payout = cash`. The whole of the period's own money is shared out, as in a VSLA.

`claim_share(member, p)` requires `p` closed and still in its slot, the member not Defaulted, and no open loan; it pays `payout × stamps_in_p / total_stamps` and advances `last_claimed_period`. Claims go in order, skipping periods that have expired. The claim window therefore runs from `close_period(p)` until period `p + 3` starts, about one period after close. A share not claimed by then rolls into the open period's cash. With `total_stamps = 0` the payout rolls over the same way. Integer-division dust stays in the slot and rolls over on expiry.

**PeriodBacklog.** If period `p − 1` is still unclosed when `p + 2` would start (an overdue loan nobody defaulted for two whole periods), entry points that need the new slot refuse with `PeriodBacklog` until someone calls `mark_default` and `close_period`. The web shows the overdue loan and offers both calls.

Forfeit is stored in the bitmap: `mark_default` clears the defaulter's paid bits for every unclaimed cycle and subtracts the counts from each affected live slot's `total_stamps` (a closed slot's `payout` is unchanged, so the remaining members' shares grow). Repaying after default closes the loan but does not restore stamps.

### Member status

| Status | Entered by | pay_dues | borrow | repay | claim_share | move | Effect on entry |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Active | `join`, `reinstate`, `join` of a Left address | yes | yes | yes | yes | yes | none |
| Suspended | `suspend` (Treasury) | yes | no | yes | yes | yes | none; `reinstate` returns to Active |

## Pilot and partners (2026-11-18)

**Why now.** The Circle's code dependencies have shipped, but nobody has yet shown that a real savings group will run its money through a tribe. The idea-validation pass (2026-10-15) said to prove that by hand before building: the onboarding wall (a wallet per member, XLM for fees and reserves, a USDC trustline, cash or mobile money in and out) sits outside the contract and may decide everything.

## Success Criteria

- A savings-only, locked DAO is created with a Circle; five wallets join, pay three cycles; one is voted a loan at the cap and one above it fails; the loan is repaid with interest into the pot; one member defaults; `close_period` shares out the period's own cash, pro-rata, excluding the defaulter; every step appears in the feed.
- **Two pots hold:** no Treasury proposal can move pot funds (only `disburse` and claims move them); `pay_dues` with `social_fee > 0` sends exactly that part to the Treasury.
- **Locked tokens hold** (full criteria in `locked-tokens.md`): on the savings-only, locked DAO, holder-to-holder `transfer`, `approve` to a non-Treasury spender, and Marketplace `list` fail with `TransfersLocked`; `move_membership` moves the record and the badge together through `agent_transfer`; `dao_transfer` plus `recover_membership` recover a lost-key member when recovery is on and fail with `RecoveryOff` when it is off.
- **The allowance holds:** with an allowance of 5, the sixth `join` fails with `AllowanceExceeded` and no dues move.
- `cargo test -p circle` covers cap, override, interest, forfeit, close, claim, and slot-reuse arithmetic; `dao-e2e` runs a full period.
- **Overlapping periods stay exact:** p+1 dues and a p+1 loan before `close_period(p)` leave payout(p) equal to p's own savings plus repayments, to the unit; a share unclaimed when p+3 starts rolls into the open period; `PeriodBacklog` fires when p-1 is unclosed as p+2 starts.
- **Renewal:** `renewal_keys` matches a ledger scan (R3 e2e), including a second `MemberPage` and a deleted loan id.

### 0A. Premise challenge

- **Real problem:** a savings group with no bank needs (1) its savings to be safe and come back to the members, and (2) credit when a member needs it. The plan delivers (2) with a capped, voted loan. It does not deliver (1): see CEO-1.
- **Do nothing:** Tribe Builder stays an auction/claim-list DAO factory; savings groups keep paper ledgers or app ledgers (DreamSave, Ledger Link) with no shared custody guarantees.
- **Finding CEO-1 (critical): members' savings are not theirs.** Premise 1 puts every member's dues in the DAO Treasury. The Treasury pays out whatever any passed proposal says, so (a) a majority can vote the pot to anything, and (b) a member gets their savings back only if a `fund_share_out` proposal passes; the plan explicitly lets a period "settle" claimable at zero when that vote never comes ("Periods, share-out, and forfeit"). In a real VSLA the share-out is the members' right, not a vote. A savings product where getting your own money back depends on a vote is not a savings product.
- **Finding CEO-2 (high): "emergency" loans take days.** Every loan is a full proposal: voting delay + voting period + queue delay, each 300 s to 30 days (`contracts/governor/src/contract.rs:402-420`), shared with every other proposal type, so a DAO cannot have fast loans and slow upgrades. The owner chose "Full DAO vote" before the cap existed; the cap now bounds what a fast path could lose. Reopened on changed assumptions, asked after CEO-1 because the answer depends on where the pot lives.
