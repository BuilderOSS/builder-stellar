## Overview

Voting is section 3 of **Build a tribe**. You choose how votes are counted and how fast decisions happen. A pace sets all the voting rules at once, and **Advanced settings** shows each rule.

## Before you start

- Complete the [Membership](/guides/create/membership) section and select **Continue to Voting**.
- It helps to know how a proposal moves from a vote to its result. See [Proposals](/concepts/proposals) and [Votes and delegation](/concepts/votes-and-delegation).

## Steps

1. Under **How are votes counted?**, keep **Token-weighted** (Default): one membership, one vote, and members can delegate their votes. **Neural Quorum Governance (NQG)** is marked Soon. It becomes available once NQG exists on Stellar.

   <Shot id="create/voting/01" alt="The How are votes counted? choices with Token-weighted selected and NQG marked Soon" />

2. Under **How fast should decisions happen?**, choose a pace. Each pace sets five rules: the wait before voting opens, how long voting stays open, the [safety delay](/reference/glossary#safety-delay), the [quorum](/reference/glossary#quorum), and the votes needed to propose.
   - **Super fast** (marked Testnet): votes open after 5 minutes and run 10 minutes, a 5-minute safety delay, 1% quorum, and 1 vote to propose. It also sets auctions to 5 minutes, with 1 minute added by a late bid. Use it to try the whole cycle on testnet.
   - **Fast**: votes open after 5 minutes and run 1 hour, a 10-minute safety delay, 5% quorum, and 1 vote to propose.
   - **Balanced** (Recommended): votes open after 1 day and run 3 days, a 1-hour safety delay, 10% quorum, and 1 vote to propose.
   - **Deliberate**: votes open after 2 days and run 7 days, a 1-day safety delay, 20% quorum, and 2 votes to propose.

   <Shot id="create/voting/02" alt="The four voting paces with Balanced selected and marked Recommended" />

3. Open **Advanced settings** to see or change each rule. If you change one, the pace shows as **Custom**.
   - **Wait before voting opens**, **How long voting stays open**, and **Safety delay before a passed vote runs**: each from 5 minutes to 30 days.
   - **Quorum: share of votes that must take part**: from 0.01% to 100%. For and Abstain votes count toward it.
   - **Votes needed to propose**: a number of tokens, not a percentage. It must be at least 1.
   - **Vote source**: shown only when the platform has approved other vote sources. It lists "The tribe's token (token-weighted)" and each approved source.

   <Shot id="create/voting/03" alt="Advanced settings open with the three timings, the quorum, and the votes needed to propose" />

   <Callout title="A vote source can't be changed later" description="If you choose an approved vote source, that contract decides every member's voting power. It is fixed when the tribe is created and can't be changed, even by a vote. Memberships still come from your tribe's token." />

4. Select **Continue to review**.

   <Shot id="create/voting/04" alt="The Review and create section opening below a completed Voting section" />

Every rule except the vote source can change later. Before launch, the [launch admin](/reference/glossary#launch-admin) changes them directly in **Manage**, under **Voting rules**. After launch, they change only by a proposal that members vote on.

Check the votes needed to propose against your founder tokens. If the founders hold fewer votes than this number, no one can make a proposal until more tokens exist. See [Set up: founders](/guides/setup/founders).

## What happens on chain

<Disclosure title="Contracts, fees, and waits">
  Nothing in this section is sent to the network yet. Your choices are saved in the draft in this browser.

  When you create the tribe, these rules are set on its Governor contract (the contract for proposals and votes): voting delay, voting period, queue delay (the safety delay), quorum in basis points (1,000 basis points is 10%), and the proposal threshold. The Governor reads voting power from the tribe's token, or from the vote source you chose.

  The contracts accept each timing from 5 minutes (300 seconds) to 30 days (2,592,000 seconds). After launch, the Governor's admin is the tribe's Treasury, so only a passed proposal can change a rule.
</Disclosure>

## If something goes wrong

- **"Voting delay must be at least 5 minutes"**, or a similar message for the voting period or queue delay. Set the timing to at least 5 minutes and no more than 30 days.
- **An error under Votes needed to propose.** Enter a whole number of 1 or more.
- **Super fast is missing.** It is offered only on testnet.
- **You don't see a Vote source field.** No other vote source is approved on this network, so votes are token-weighted.
- **The vote source shows "No longer approved".** The platform has withdrawn its approval of the source in your draft. Choose another source, or the tribe's token.
