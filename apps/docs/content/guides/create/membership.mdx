## Overview

Membership is section 2 of **Build a tribe**. You choose how people become members, and who can hold a membership. Each membership is a token: an NFT on Stellar that carries one vote. See [Membership tokens](/concepts/membership-tokens).

## Before you start

- Complete the [Identity](/guides/create/identity) section and select **Continue to Membership**.
- Prices, timings, and membership rules start with defaults. You can change any of them later by a vote. Payment assets are the exception: see the note in step 3.

## Steps

1. Under **How do people join?**, choose one option. Each option decides which modules your tribe is created with. A module is a contract that sells or gives out memberships. See [Modules](/concepts/modules).
   - **Daily auction** (Recommended): one new token is auctioned at a time, and each sale goes to the [treasury](/reference/glossary#treasury). Creates an auction.
   - **Auction + resale**: auctions, and members can also resell their tokens to others. Creates an auction and a market.
   - **Fixed-price sales**: the tribe sells tokens at a set price from quick-buy pools. Members open each pool by vote after launch. Creates a market.
   - **Invite only**: no sales. People claim their tokens from invite lists the tribe publishes. Creates a claims module.

   <Shot id="create/membership/01" alt="The How do people join? choices with Daily auction selected and marked Recommended" />

2. Under **Who can hold a membership?**, choose one option.
   - **Open membership**: members can sell, give, or [delegate](/reference/glossary#delegate) their tokens freely.
   - **Members-only club**: a membership changes hands only with the tribe's approval, votes stay among members, and the tribe can move any membership by vote, for example when a member loses access to their wallet.

   <Shot id="create/membership/02" alt="The Who can hold a membership? choices with Open membership selected" />

3. Open **Advanced settings** if you want to change the defaults. The groups you see depend on your choice in step 1.
   - **Auctions**: **Bids are in** (XLM or USDC), **Starting price** (the lowest first bid, at least 0.0001, up to 7 decimal places; default 1), **Each auction lasts** (5 minutes to 30 days; default 1 day), and **A late bid adds** (a bid near the end extends the auction by this much, up to 1 day; default 15 minutes).
   - **Market**: **Prices are in** (XLM or USDC) and **Fee on resales** (paid by the seller, from 0% to 25%; default 5%).
   - **Membership rules**: the three rules behind step 2, one checkbox each. A mix that matches neither option is shown as your own mix.
   - **Claim lists**: **Let members claim tokens from published lists** adds a claims module to any option. Paid lists use the auction's asset. With no auction, choose it in **Paid lists are in**.

   <Shot id="create/membership/03" alt="Advanced settings open, showing the Auctions fields, the membership rules checkboxes, and the claim lists option" />

   <Callout title="Payment assets are set when you create the tribe" description="Check Bids are in, Prices are in, and Paid lists are in before you create the tribe. The app treats these assets as fixed, and launch is refused if a module no longer uses the asset chosen at creation." />

4. Select **Continue to Voting**.

   <Shot id="create/membership/04" alt="The Voting section opening below a completed Membership section" />

Modules you leave out can be added later: directly in Setup, or by vote after launch. See [Set up: contracts](/guides/setup/contracts). If you turn off every way to join, only founder tokens exist until a vote adds one. You mint at least one founder token in Setup before launch. See [Set up: founders](/guides/setup/founders).

## What happens on chain

<Disclosure title="Contracts, fees, and waits">
  Nothing in this section is sent to the network yet. Your choices are saved in the draft in this browser.

  When you create the tribe, the factory contract (the Manager) deploys only the modules your choice needs: an auction contract, a market contract, a claims contract (ClaimMinter), or a combination. It records each module's payment asset. At launch, the Manager checks that each module still uses that asset.

  The three membership rules are settings on the membership token contract: transfers locked, members-only delegation, and DAO recovery. All three are off for **Open membership** and on for **Members-only club**. After launch they change only by a proposal.

  Auctions do not start, and the market does not open, at creation. You choose that when you launch. See [Launch your tribe](/guides/setup/launch).
</Disclosure>

## If something goes wrong

- **"Use an amount from 0.0001 with up to 7 decimal places"**. Check the **Starting price**. Use a dot for decimals and no more than 7 digits after it.
- **"The marketplace fee is at most 2,500 bps (25%)."** Lower the **Fee on resales** to 25% or less.
- **A message says members can't resell on the market.** This appears when you choose a market and memberships are locked. The market can still sell new tokens from pools the tribe opens by vote.
- **The review says "only founder tokens exist until a vote adds a way to join".** You chose **Invite only** and turned off claim lists. Turn claim lists back on, or choose another option.
