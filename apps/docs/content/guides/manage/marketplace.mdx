## Overview

**Manage → Market** holds the tribe-level settings of the market: the payment asset, the resale fee, pausing, and quick-buy pools. Every change here requires DAO governance. Each button adds an action to your proposal draft, and nothing is signed on this page. For buying and selling memberships, see [Sell a token](/guides/marketplace/sell/) and [Buy a token](/guides/marketplace/buy/).

## Before you start

- The tribe must have a market and must have launched. Before launch the page says "The market opens at launch", and these changes can be proposed only after launch.
- Connect your wallet on the tribe's network and sign in. Testnet is the live network today. Any connected wallet can add these actions to a draft. Whether you can submit the proposal is checked when you do. See [Write a proposal](/guides/proposals/write/).
- The changes apply only if members vote for them and the proposal is executed.

## Steps

1. **Open Market.** Choose **Manage** in the tribe's navigation, then **Market**. The page is headed **Manage the marketplace**. The notice at the top says whether the market is open or paused, the asset prices are in, and the resale fee.

   <Shot id="manage/marketplace/01" alt="The Manage the marketplace page with the notice showing the market is open, prices in XLM and the resale fee" />

2. **Change the payment asset or the resale fee.** Under **Payment, fee and pause changes**, use **Change the market payment asset** to choose the asset buyers pay in: XLM or USDC. Use **Change the resale fee** to set the fee on each resale, from 0% to 25%. The seller pays the fee, and it goes to the tribe's [Treasury](/reference/glossary#treasury). Choose **Add to proposal** on the setting you changed. Listings already open keep their asset and fee.

   <Shot id="manage/marketplace/02" alt="The Change the resale fee setting with a new percentage and its Add to proposal button" />

3. **Pause or reopen the market.** **Pause the market** stops sales until a vote reopens it. **Reopen the market** lets sales start again. Choose **Add to proposal** on the one you need.

   <Shot id="manage/marketplace/03" alt="The Pause the market setting with its Add to proposal button" />

4. **Propose a quick-buy pool.** A pool sells new tokens at a fixed price. Under **Propose a quick-buy pool**, fill in **Price per token**, **Tokens in the pool**, **Maximum per wallet**, **Maximum sales per period**, **Period (hours)** and **Closes at (local time)**. Choose a closing time that leaves enough time for the vote and execution. Then choose **Add the pool to proposal**. It joins your draft as one item with two steps: raising the market's mint allowance, then opening the pool.

   <Shot id="manage/marketplace/04" alt="The Propose a quick-buy pool form filled in, with the Each token sold adds a vote warning beneath it" />

5. **Close or sweep a pool.** Under **Propose closing or sweeping a pool**, choose a pool from the list or enter its number in **Pool**. **Add closing to proposal** stops the pool's sales and returns the tokens it never minted to the market's mint allowance. **Add sweeping to proposal** moves a closed pool's unsold tokens to the Treasury. A pool must be closed before it can be swept.

   <Shot id="manage/marketplace/05" alt="The Propose closing or sweeping a pool section with a pool selected and its two buttons" />

6. **Submit the proposal.** Open your draft from **Review proposal** at the top of the page, add a title and a reason, then submit it. See [Write a proposal](/guides/proposals/write/).

   <Shot id="manage/marketplace/06" alt="The proposal draft bar showing actions waiting for a vote and the Review proposal button" />

<Callout title="Each token sold adds a vote" description="A pool larger than the current voting supply could let buyers outvote today's members. The pool form shows the largest share one wallet could buy. Keep pools small and the per-wallet limit low." />

The same page also has a section for the Treasury to buy a listed token. See [Treasury purchases](/guides/marketplace/treasury-purchases/).

## What happens on chain

<Disclosure title="Contracts, fees and waits">
  These actions call the tribe's Marketplace contract: `set_payment_asset`, `set_secondary_fee_bps`, `pause`, `unpause`, `open_pool`, `close_pool` and `sweep_pool`. After launch the Treasury is the market's admin, so each call runs only as part of a proposal that passes and is executed.

  The resale fee is stored in basis points, at most 2,500 (25%). A new fee or payment asset applies to listings made afterwards.

  While the market is paused, nobody can list a token, buy a listing, open a pool, or buy from a pool. Sellers can still cancel their listings.

  A pool mints new tokens against the market's mint allowance, so the proposal raises that allowance by the pool's size before opening it. Proceeds from pool sales go to the Treasury.

  The wallet that submits each proposal step pays the Stellar network fee in XLM. Tribe Builder takes no fee. Settings, pools and sales are public on chain.
</Disclosure>

## If something goes wrong

- **"Connect your wallet. Pools can be proposed once the tribe has launched."** Connect and sign in, and check that the tribe has launched.
- **"The marketplace is paused."** A pool can't open while the market is paused. Add a **Reopen the market** action before the pool in the same proposal, or wait until a reopen proposal is executed.
- **"The market has to take XLM or USDC before a pool can be priced."** Change the market payment asset first.
- **"Choose a closing time in the future, allowing time for the vote and execution."** Pick a later closing time. If the time passes before the proposal is executed, opening the pool fails.
- **"Pool #3 is still open: close it first, then sweep it."** Close the pool in one proposal. Sweep it once it is closed.
- **"The market didn't load."** Reload the page.
