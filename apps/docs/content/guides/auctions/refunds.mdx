## Overview

When someone outbids you, your bid comes back to your wallet automatically. You only need this guide if the automatic refund couldn't be sent. The amount is then held for you by the Auction contract until you claim it.

## Before you start

- Connect the wallet that placed the bid, on the tribe's network.
- Keep a small amount of XLM for the network fee.
- Make sure your wallet can receive the auction's payment asset. For an asset other than XLM, that means it holds a trustline for that asset.

## How refunds work

There are three cases:

- **You are outbid.** Your full bid goes back to your wallet in the same transaction as the new, higher bid. This is the usual case.
- **The automatic transfer fails.** For example, your wallet can't receive the payment asset at that moment. The auction carries on, and the amount is held for you as a waiting refund. It stays held until you claim it.
- **The tribe cancels the auction.** A tribe can cancel a running auction by proposal. The top bid is refunded in the same way, and the token goes to the tribe's [Treasury](/reference/glossary#treasury).

The winning bid is not refunded. When the auction is settled, it goes to the Treasury.

## Steps

1. **Open the Auction page.** With your wallet connected, the page checks for a waiting refund. If there is one, it shows **Refund waiting**, with the amount, for example "12.5 XLM is yours to claim".

2. **Choose Claim refund.** Your wallet opens to show the transaction. Review it and sign.

3. **Check the result.** The page reports **Refund claim sent**, then **Refund claimed**. The notice disappears, and the whole amount is back in your wallet.

## What happens on chain

<Disclosure title="Contracts, fees and waits">
  When a new bid is placed, the Auction contract tries to transfer the previous top bid back to that bidder. If the transfer succeeds, it emits `BidRefunded`. If it fails, the contract adds the amount to a pending refund for that bidder and emits `RefundDeferred`. A failed transfer moves nothing, so no funds are lost.

  **Claim refund** calls `withdraw_refund` on the Auction contract. It needs your signature, takes no amount and pays out the whole pending balance to your wallet. It emits `RefundWithdrawn`. You can check your balance at any time with `pending_refund`.

  A pending refund is stored as your own entry on chain. Like other stored data on Stellar, it has a lifetime and can be archived if no one touches it for a long time. The amount stays recorded. If your entry was archived, the app restores it first, which asks for one extra wallet signature and its own network fee. See [Storage and renewal](/concepts/storage-and-renewal/).

  You pay the Stellar network fee in XLM. Refunds are public on chain.
</Disclosure>

## If something goes wrong

- **"We couldn't check for a refund."** The page couldn't read the Auction contract. Choose **Check again** before you assume there is no refund.
- **No notice appears, but you expected a refund.** Check that the connected wallet is the one that placed the bid. If you were outbid normally, the refund was already sent to that wallet. Look for it in your wallet's history.
- **The claim fails again.** Your wallet may still be unable to receive the payment asset. Add the trustline for that asset in your wallet, then try again. The amount stays held until a claim succeeds.
- **"No deferred refund to claim."** The refund was already claimed. Refresh the page.
