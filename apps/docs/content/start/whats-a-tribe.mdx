A tribe is a group of people with a shared treasury who decide together. In technical terms, it is a DAO (decentralised autonomous organisation) on Stellar.

A tribe has five parts. Each part below starts in plain words. The DAO term follows for readers who already know it.

## Members

Each member holds a token. The token gives you membership and a vote on what the tribe does. If you hold 3 tokens, you have 3 votes.

The DAO term: each membership is an NFT with one vote. Read more in [Membership tokens](/concepts/membership-tokens).

## Treasury

The treasury is the money the tribe holds together. When people pay for a membership, the payment goes to the treasury. Anyone can add more.

The treasury pays out only when a proposal passes. Every payment in and out is public, so anyone can see where the funds went.

The DAO term: an onchain treasury contract that no single person controls. Read more in [The Treasury](/concepts/treasury).

## Proposals

A proposal is a request that members vote on. For example, it can pay someone for work, sell a token, or change a setting. A proposal lists its actions in advance. If it passes, those actions run exactly as written, and nothing else.

The DAO term: governance proposals with onchain actions. Any wallet can execute one that is ready. Read more in [Proposals](/concepts/proposals).

## Votes

Members vote For, Against, or Abstain on each proposal. You can also choose another person to vote for you. That person is your [delegate](/reference/glossary#delegate). You keep your tokens, and you can change your delegate at any time.

A proposal needs enough votes to pass. This minimum is the [quorum](/reference/glossary#quorum). After a proposal passes, there is a [safety delay](/reference/glossary#safety-delay) before it can run, so members can review what will happen.

The DAO term: token-weighted voting with delegation, a quorum, and a safety delay. Read more in [Votes and delegation](/concepts/votes-and-delegation).

## Joining

A tribe chooses how people join. It can use one or more of these ways:

- **An auction.** One token is auctioned at a time, and the highest bid wins.
- **A market.** The tribe sells tokens at a set price, and members can resell the tokens they hold, if the tribe allows it.
- **A claim list.** The tribe publishes a list of people who can claim a token.

To join, you need a Stellar wallet and some XLM, the Stellar network's currency. Each Stellar transaction has a small network fee, paid in XLM.

## What a tribe is not

- A tribe is not run by Tribe Builder. Once it launches, only its members can change it, by vote.
- A tribe is not a company or other legal entity by itself. A tribe that wants one can prepare a [legal entity export](/guides/legal/export).
- A membership token is a vote and a record of membership. It is not a promise of any financial return.
